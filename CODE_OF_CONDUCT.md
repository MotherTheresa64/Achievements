# Code of Conduct

This repository is a small public engineering and workflow lab. Contributions, bug reports, reviews, and technical discussion are welcome when they are constructive and made in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code or ideas.
- Critique the work rather than the person.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on their stated purpose.
- Share enough context for others to reproduce or evaluate a claim.
- Protect private information, credentials, and security-sensitive details.
- Be transparent about automated or AI-assisted contributions when that context materially affects review.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, deliberate disruption, spam, impersonation, fabricated contribution history, and attempts to manipulate repository metrics through deceptive activity are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove disruptive content and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and useful. Security-sensitive reports should follow `SECURITY.md` instead of being posted publicly.
